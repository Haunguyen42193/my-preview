# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026  
**Application:** NVH_N8N (`https://github.com/Haunguyen42193/my-preview`)

## 1. Information We Collect
When you authorize **NVH_N8N** with your TikTok account, we only access the minimum OAuth tokens and basic profile information (`user.info.basic`, `video.upload`, `video.publish`) required to upload videos to your account.

## 2. How We Use Information
- OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the operator's machine solely to authenticate requests to the TikTok Content Posting API.
- We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal data or tokens with any third parties.

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion
You may revoke **NVH_N8N**'s access at any time from your TikTok account security settings, and delete locally stored tokens from your environment configuration.

## 4. Contact
For privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, please contact the repository owner via GitHub at `https://github.com/Haunguyen42193/my-preview`.
